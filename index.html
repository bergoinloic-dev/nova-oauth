NOVA VORTEX YouTube

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channels of one independent creator.

NOVA VORTEX YouTube is a personal, self-hosted tool used by its operator, Loïc Bergoin, to manage his own YouTube channels. It runs on the operator's own computer. It is not a public service and it is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the operator has produced and approved to the operator's own YouTube channels, as private videos, optionally with a scheduled publication date.
  • Sets the title, description, thumbnail and captions of those videos.
  • Reads the identity of the connected channel to make sure a video goes to the intended channel.
  • Reads the statistics (YouTube Analytics) of the operator's own channels, read-only.

It uses Google sign-in (OAuth) only to access the operator's own YouTube channels, with the operator's consent.
